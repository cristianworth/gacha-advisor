## Data policy

- **Owned** means the user explicitly reported acquisition/ownership, or supplied a roster.
- **Discussed** means a character, weapon, or team appeared in a question; it is **not** evidence of ownership.
- Missing or unlisted gear, constellations, resources, or teams are **unknown**, not zero or absent.
- Builds, stats, weapons, rotations and patch details may change; use current sources for claims about optimality.
- Preserve the user's character names and notation if a source cannot confidently resolve an alias. Do not silently invent an official alias or convert an ownership mention into verified gameplay data.
- Public sources verify game mechanics, not private account ownership. An unresolved name remains user-reported; its kit, element and official alias remain unknown until identified.
- Explicit newer user updates/corrections override the corresponding older field. If statements conflict without a clear update, retain the conflict and ask only when it affects the recommendation.
- **Category-report preference — explicitly requested on 2026-10-15:** whenever the user says "I own all 4-star weapons/characters" or makes another broad ownership statement, research the individual names in that game's stated category and enumerate them in this Markdown snapshot. Keep the user's report as the ownership source; cite dated catalogs for names and category membership. Record the researched scope and unresolved gaps instead of leaving only "all" or inventing entries. Preserve explicit exceptions, variants and ranks; do not infer copies, refinements, superimpositions, builds or ownership of later releases from the category statement.
